Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa with Abuja as its capital city and Lagos as its major commercial and financial centre. It is home to over 250 ethnic groups, including the Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo. English is the official language, while many indigenous languages, such as Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo, are widely spoken. Nigeria is also famous for its rich cultural heritage, Afrobeats music, Nollywood films, and delicious traditional foods such as Jollof rice.
